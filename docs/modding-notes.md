Natural observations do not certify every siege/raid/war combination. Direct public murder exposure before observation remains unsupported; no omniscient fallback is permitted.

Consult the relevant topic before relying on a capability. Update validated reusable findings in the same change, with status, tested version, portable source/procedure, implications and limits. If no durable finding was made, record that in the change/report instead of manufacturing a note. Machine setup, attempts, raw logs, reports, screenshots, saves and profile backups remain in ignored `.local/MODLOG.md` or `.local/automation/`.
